Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Best_Products ("the App") is a private tool used by its owner to publish promotional videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens of the account owner, used only to publish videos to that account. They are stored locally on the owner's computer.
  • Product information (titles, images, prices) used to create the videos.

What we do not do

The App does not collect personal data of TikTok viewers or other users, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings and are deleted from the owner's computer on request.
